# SHUTDOWN v4 — Painted industrial thriller

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Concept exploration only; these images are not screenshots of implemented gameplay. Crew and Overseer Mode remain concept features.

## Shared direction

Use case: stylized-concept / ui-mockup. Create one polished landscape 16:9 concept screen for SHUTDOWN, a mobile stealth-horror game set in an adaptive industrial facility that hears the player's real microphone. Art direction for the entire eleven-screen set: hand-painted industrial thriller. Authored 2.5D gouache-painted miniature environments with confident angular contours, deliberately simplified shapes, matte enamel surfaces, selective worn edges and drybrush marks. A restrained palette of petrol blue #23474C, faded teal #507C79, dirty ivory #DED7BC, mustard amber #DEA33A, near-black indigo #142127; vermilion red only for danger. Pool warm practical light against cool hard shadows. Physical, slightly uncanny, tangible, soulful. Strong negative space and clear visual hierarchy. Machinery has believable functions, thick curved pipes, analogue dials and cast-metal housings; every detail purposeful. Avoid generic sci-fi neon, purple-blue gradients, ubiquitous glow, floating particles, fog obscuring gameplay, ornamental greebles, glossy plastic, stock asset aesthetics, voxel characters, excessive distress texture and unreadable pseudo-text. The operator is a slight human figure in mustard work overalls, dark gloves and boots, a cream protective hood with an opaque dark visor, a compact rectangular battery pack; expressive cautious posture. The Warden is tall and thin with asymmetrical jointed steel limbs, petrol-painted shoulder casing, a cream horizontal instrument head with a single vermilion rectangular slit, no face and no weapon. Isometric gameplay camera, physically connected walkable spaces. UI is bespoke industrial wayfinding: clean narrow grotesk headlines and legible monospaced instrument labels, ivory ink on deep petrol panels, fine rules, amber rectangular action buttons. Layout should be usable on a landscape phone, large enough text, no device frame, no watermark. This is an art concept, not a claim of implemented functionality.
